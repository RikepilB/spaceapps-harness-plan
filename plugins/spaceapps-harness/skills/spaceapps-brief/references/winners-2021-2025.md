# Global winners, 2021-2025

Ten awards per year, one project each. Sources: NASA STEM engagement press releases and
spaceappschallenge.org awards pages.

## 2025 (114,000+ participants · 551 events · 167 countries · 11,500+ submissions)

| Award | Project | Location | Challenge | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Best Use of Science | SpaceGenes+ | Germany | Build a Space Biology Knowledge Engine | Interactive dashboard on how radiation and microgravity jointly affect astronaut health at the molecular level |
| Best Use of Data | Resonant Exoplanets | United States | A World Away: Hunting for Exoplanets with AI | Ingests telescope and satellite data, auto-analyses for exoplanets and possible biosignatures |
| Best Use of Technology | Twisters (SkySense) | Mexico | Will It Rain On My Parade? | Earth-observation data plus AI for ultra-local personalized weather prediction |
| Galactic Impact | Astro Sweepers | Universal Event | Commercializing LEO | Orbital-debris compliance and risk platform; ingests public orbital data, generates Debris Assessment Software reports |
| Best Mission Concept | PureFlow | Brazil | Your Home in Space: The Habitat Layout Creator | Systems-engineering platform to design, 3D-model and validate habitats against real space-weather threats |
| Most Inspirational | Photonics Odyssey | India | Commercializing LEO | Satellite internet reframed as sovereign national infrastructure via a phased-array antenna approach |
| Best Use of Storytelling | HerCode Space | Universal Event | Stellar Stories | NASA data and heliophysics taught to children through illustration and narrative |
| Global Connection | Gaia+LEO | United States | Commercializing LEO | Mixed-integer optimization co-designing orbital and terrestrial data-centre networks |
| Art & Technology | Zumorroda-X | Egypt | NASA Farm Navigators | Mini-games putting players in a farmer's position adapting to heat waves and flooding |
| Local Impact | QUEÑARIS | Peru | BloomWatch | Native microorganisms + NASA satellite data + drones + AI to accelerate tree growth and target reforestation |

Note: three of the ten came from one challenge (Commercializing LEO) and two came
through the Universal Event.
